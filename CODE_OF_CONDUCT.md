# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

OTelux contributors, maintainers, and community members commit to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or level of participation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Welcome questions and make technical decisions transparent.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's identity, communications, or telemetry without permission.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes promptly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory conduct.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, sustained disruption, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing private information, credentials, telemetry, or communications without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository activity, project communication, community events, and public interactions where someone is representing OTelux.

## Reporting

Do not put sensitive conduct reports in a public issue.

This repository is currently private and does not yet have an independent conduct recipient. Public launch is blocked until a trusted recipient who is not the sole repository administrator is named here with a verified confidential channel. Reports involving a maintainer must bypass that maintainer and go directly to the independent recipient.

Include only the information needed to understand the incident. Reports are handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. The maintainer will acknowledge a report, assess immediate safety needs, hear affected parties where appropriate, and communicate the outcome to the reporter when possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, issue a correction or warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation. The response will be proportional to the conduct, its impact, prior behavior, and risk to the community. Maintainers with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves when another suitable reviewer is available.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. Deliberately false or malicious reports may themselves violate this policy.
